Overview
**DJ V-SCRATCH LIVE** is a high-performance standalone DJ workstation engineered in pure C++20 and JUCE. Designed for professional turntablists, club performers, and audio purists, it combines physical-grade vinyl scratch emulation, 3D spatial audio placement, and sample-accurate video/karaoke synchronization.

Key Modules & Architecture

1. **Dual Vinyl Turntable Engine (`DJ V-SCRATCH LIVE`)**
   - High-precision tactile vinyl physics with zero-latency response.
   - Interactive needle drops, continuous waveform tracking, and multi-point cueing.
   - 3-Band isolator kill EQs, studio gain trim, and optical crossfader curves.
   - **Dedicated Tablet Touch Mode** with enlarged touch targets, frameless borderless UI, and optimized touch ergonomics.

2. **V-CHOPPER Sample & Slicing Suite**
   - Real-time beat grid and transient slicing.
   - Instant randomizer and live re-arranger for remixing on stage.
   - Glitch-free audio scrubbing and stereo bed routing.

3. **V-SPHERE 3D Spatial Audio Engine**
   - 360° Binaural HRTF acoustic positioning with elevation and azimuth control.
   - Dynamic orbital movement and psychoacoustic room reflections.
   - Audiophile headphone virtualization and multichannel surround master output.

4. **V-VIDEO & A/V PTS Tracking Engine**
   - Sample-accurate master clock synchronization: video frames and karaoke subtitles are strictly slaved to the DSP audio stream.
   - Dynamic rate scaling with pitch fader movement and turntable scratching.
   - Dual-screen output for DJ booth monitoring and audience club projectors.
   - Timed CDG and LRC karaoke lyrics support.

5. **Integrated Studio Tools**
   - **Center Mixer ("Herzstück")**: Optical faders, LED peak meters, and headphone cue split.
   - **Spectral Waveform Zoom**: Color-coded frequency-separated waveforms.
   - **High-Speed Crate Manager**: Instant audio indexing, key matching, and BPM calculation.
